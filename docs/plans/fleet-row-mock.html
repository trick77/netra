Fleet row — before / after

The same five hosts rendered twice. Palette, type scale and spacing are netra's own tokens, copied out of index.css.

Before — today on master

Three sizes of type in one row: the percentages at 15px, everything annotating them at 12px, and the traffic rates at 14px — a step that appears nowhere else on the row, which is the thing that reads as too large. The identity column carries a variable-width chip whose word is the same handful of words all the way down the page.

After

Two sizes now. 15px for the readings; 12px for everything that annotates one — the location line, "of 8 cores", the traffic rates, and Last seen. The identity column carries one glyph at one width on every row. warning and critical differ by shape, not only by hue: the two hues measure ΔE 2.2 under deuteranopia, so a triangle in two colours would be one mark to a reader who cannot separate them. Each glyph carries its severity as an aria-label, so the word is still there for a screen reader. offline and never seen are gone entirely — the red hostname and the Last seen column already say it, and a "critical" derived from a silent host's last known disk reading would claim to describe this minute.
